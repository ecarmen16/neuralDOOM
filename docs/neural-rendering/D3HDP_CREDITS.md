# D3HDP BFG Lite credits and source

D3HDP BFG Lite (Doom 3 Essential HD Pack BFG Lite) is created and maintained by **H3llBaron**, building on the Doom 3 modding community's work. neuralDOOM did not create these textures, models or other pack content.

- [BFG Lite project](https://www.moddb.com/mods/d3hdp-bfg-lite)
- [Original BFG Lite download](https://www.moddb.com/mods/d3hdp-bfg-lite/downloads/d3hdp-bfg-lite)
- [Parent D3HDP project and acknowledgments](https://www.moddb.com/mods/d3hdp-doom-3-essential-hd-pack/downloads/d3hdp-doom-3-essential-hd-pack-v20)

The parent project's published acknowledgments name Wulfen, Monoxead, Sikkpin, Clear_strelok, Arl, darklord34, snapshot_, warezeater, Phrozo, Dafama2k7, Quake4ever2007, Alfeos, Defunkt and the active community. This records those project-level acknowledgments, not a claim that every contributor authored a particular BFG Lite asset. Original Doom assets belong to their respective rights holders.

The downloaded BFG Lite readme is preserved unchanged beside this notice (`Readme.pdf` in the July 2026 release; `Readme.txt` in the older inspected layout). It contains installation instructions and release history, but no full contributor list. This additional notice supplies creator attribution and links to the published acknowledgments.

The installer downloads the pack from its original publisher or imports a selected original ZIP. Pack assets are not bundled with neuralDOOM releases or committed to its repository. This notice is attribution, not a grant of redistribution rights or a claim of endorsement.
